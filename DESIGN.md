# NightTroop Design

Night**Troop** hosts many (10kish) Night**Scout** servers. 
The main goal is to reduce the complexity of setting up NightScout to a single **FREE** click.

# On Boarding

1. Go to nighttroop.t1d.nz
2. Click the "Create User" button
3. Copy the **User Data** and store it somewhere safe

### Getting data into night troop

Getting the BGL and treatment data into Nightscout should be pretty easy

#### PWD Phone

1. Copy the Nightscout Compatabile URL and Password from the **User Data**
2. Copy PWD Setup Person With Diabetes (**PWD**) phone with information 

#### Dexcom/Nightscout/Libre Sync

1. Login with Admin password and TOTP token from the **User Data**
2. In the **Settings Menu** click the Sync settings

3. Fill in Dexcom Username and Password  

3. Fill in the Nightscout URL and Token

4. Fill in the Libre information

# Sharing/Devices

In the **Settings Menu** go into "identities and users"

1. Click the device you want to add and copy the information give to the device.
